## Why

Eight decisions block Horizon 1. Most of them are not technical — they are about what the product *is*, what money moves, and what risk the business accepts. Guessing at them means building something that has to be torn out.

**D-01 is the sharpest.** Everything about the barber booking — the single most distinctive feature in the project — depends on how Kimani actually takes bookings today, and nobody has asked him.

## What

Every open decision in [DECISION-REGISTER.md](../DECISION-REGISTER.md) carries an answer, a date and a name.

## Not included

The advice items **A-01 to A-07**. Those need a solicitor, an accountant or a ratings consultant, and are tracked separately. Do not let anybody — human or AI — invent those answers.

## Steps

1. Read `DECISION-REGISTER.md`, open section
2. Work through D-01 to D-08. Each has a recommendation; agreeing with it is a complete answer
3. Record answer, date and who decided
4. Where a decision was *rejected*, record why — that is worth more later than the decision itself
5. Move each to the Decided table

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] D-01 through D-08 each have an answer, a date and a name
- [ ] Any rejected recommendation has its reasoning recorded
- [ ] A-01 to A-07 have a named person responsible for obtaining advice

## Verification

Read the register. No open rows in the blocking table.
