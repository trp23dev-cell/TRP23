| **D-W06** | **Real-world rewards need a funding authority** | **Invariant: gameplay cannot manufacture unlimited real-world monetary liability.** Anything producing real-world economic value must be backed by an explicit funded source — promotional budget, partner funding, allocated inventory, margin, limited pool. | **D-W07** | **Digital entitlement ≠ physical fulfilment** | Digital purchase (TC) → entitlement, **no stock decrement, no shipment**. | **D-W08** | **The player has a Phone** | An **information, communication and navigation** surface: map, messages, missions/case file, drops, contacts, wallet/account info. *The phone informs; the world is where the player acts.* It may show a bank balance — banking happens at the bank. | **D-W20** | **Starter home location unresolved** | The home and THE COME UP share a location (D-W13), but **the actual Lincoln building is not selected and must not be invented or canonised.** The "flat above a chip shop" is a tone placeholder only. | **D-W21** | **Stakes without violence** | TRP23 needs jeopardy; jeopardy is not physical danger. Recommend Blender-authored base bodies at `TrapCharacterScale`, one shared Humanoid skeleton, garments per archetype. **Crowd variety (U16a) is the real trade and is decided separately** | 9 Aug | AI, **pending owner** |

Canon stays 1.80 m / 1.68 m eye, real metres, Lincoln authoritative | 10 Aug | Kimani |
